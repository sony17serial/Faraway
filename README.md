## 📖 Overview
<a id="overview"></a>

**Faraway** is a mesmerizing arcade game for Windows that immerses players in a minimalist cosmic adventure. Developed by Annapurna Interactive, the **latest version** invites you to control a shooting star navigating through the galaxy while skillfully avoiding its imminent demise. With its engaging puzzle-solving mechanics, players harness cosmic energy by forming constellations to keep the star alive, offering a unique blend of strategy and creativity.

Experience 16 diverse puzzle stages, each featuring procedurally generated elements based on player actions. The **full functionality** of Faraway ensures a fresh experience with every playthrough. With a visually pleasing minimalist art style and a relaxing atmosphere, Faraway is perfect for those looking to explore the cosmos while engaging in strategic gameplay.
